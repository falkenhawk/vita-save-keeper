# Save Keeper

Save Keeper is free, open-source homebrew for the PS Vita and PS TV. It backs up your Vita, PSP and homebrew
saves and syncs them through your own Google Drive, so you can go back to an older save or pick it up on
another console.

- [download the latest release](https://github.com/falkenhawk/vita-save-keeper/releases/latest)
- [Google Drive setup guide](https://github.com/falkenhawk/vita-save-keeper/blob/master/docs/google-drive-setup.md)
- [source code on GitHub](https://github.com/falkenhawk/vita-save-keeper)
- [privacy policy](privacy.html)

## Google Drive access

Save Keeper asks Google for one permission, `drive.file`, which covers only the files the app created itself:
a `PSV Saves` folder holding your backups. It cannot see anything else in your Drive or your Google account.
Your console talks directly to Google. There is no Save Keeper server.
